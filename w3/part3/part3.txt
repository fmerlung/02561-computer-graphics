Translation matrix: Used to center the cube vertices. Since the vertices are defined in range (0, 1), 
they need to be moved for the object to have its center in the origin.

Y-Axis rotation matrix: Used to rotate the model around the Y-axis. 

X-Axis rotation matrix: Used to rotate the model around the X-axis.

View matrix: Used to transform world cooridnates into camera coordinates.

Project matrix: Used to transform camera coordinates into clip space.

Cube 1 (one-point): Centering translation -> View -> projection
M = P * V * T

Cube 2 (two-point): Centering translation -> Rotation Y -> View -> Projection
M = P * V * R_y * T

Cube 3 (three-point): Centering translation -> Rotation Y -> Rotation X -> View -> Projection
M = P * V * R_x * R_y * T
