Please use a browser that supports HTML5 canvas. Vertex normals are obtained from the OBJ file, using the "vn" entries. Normals are then transformed to world space, so they can be compared witht the light position.
The smoothness of the surface relies on the interpolation of the vertex normals across the surface. Phong shading is used to compute the lighting per pixel, and despite the low poly count of the model, it ends up appearing quite smooth.
